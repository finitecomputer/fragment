# The wire

Generated by `cargo xtask wire` from crates/proto (its `src/routes.rs` and
the types it names): edit those, never this file. [docs/api.md](../api.md)
says what each route means, who may call it and its limits;
`crates/proto/wire.lock` holds what an older client relies on
(`xtask/src/wire/lock.rs`). The same as OpenAPI 3.1: [openapi.json](openapi.json).

## Routes

### fragments

A signer's list of the fragments they hold a role on, and their own view of each.

#### `fragments.archive`

`PUT /api/fragments/{name}/archived`: Archive or unarchive a fragment in the signer's own list.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Body: [`SetArchived`](#setarchived)
- Answer: [`Archived`](#archived)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `fragments.dock`

`PUT /api/fragments/{name}/dock`: Put a fragment on the signer's dock, or take it off.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Body: [`SetDock`](#setdock)
- Answer: [`Docked`](#docked)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `fragments.list`

`GET /api/fragments`: The signer's fragments, a row each.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Query `for` (`string`, optional): An agent's: the identity it acts for. The rows are the fragments that identity holds a role on where the agent or its owner is a member, each with the role the agent acts with there, `owned` on that identity's own, and no `listed`.
- Answer: [`FragmentList`](#fragmentlist)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 401 `unauthenticated`; 403 `forbidden`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`, `registry_unavailable`

#### `fragments.pin`

`PUT /api/fragments/{name}/pinned`: Pin or unpin a fragment in the signer's sidebar.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Body: [`SetPinned`](#setpinned)
- Answer: [`Pinned`](#pinned)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `fragments.seen`

`PUT /api/fragments/{name}/seen`: The signer has seen a fragment's messages: its row counts none unread.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Answer: [`Seen`](#seen)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `fragments.status`

`GET /api/f/{name}/status`: A fragment's status, as the caller's role sees it.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session

#### `fragments.watch`

`GET /api/fragments/watch`: A socket that says when the signer's list, or the invites waiting on them, change.

### channels

A fragment's channels: its records, read, posted and drafted.

#### `channels.draft`

`PUT /api/f/{name}/channels/{channel}/draft`: Show a record as it is written to the channel's live readers; never stored.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Body: [`PutDraft`](#putdraft)
- Answer: [`Done`](#done)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 403 `forbidden`; 404 `not_found`; 413 `too_large`; 429 `rate_limited`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `channels.post`

`POST /api/f/{name}/channels/{channel}`: Append a `message` record as the poster.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Body: [`PostRecord`](#postrecord)
- Answer: [`Posted`](#posted)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 402 `budget_used_up`; 403 `forbidden`; 404 `not_found`; 409 `conflicting_body`; 413 `too_large`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

#### `channels.read`

`GET /api/f/{name}/channels/{channel}`: A page of a channel's records, always ascending.

- Credentials: NIP-98, the shell's session
- Query `after` (`integer`, optional): Read forward from past this seq (0 when neither cursor is named).
- Query `before` (`integer`, optional): Read the records just below this seq.
- Query `limit` (`integer`, optional): At most `CHANNEL_PAGE`.
- Answer: [`ChannelPage`](#channelpage)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 400 `invalid_request`; 401 `unauthenticated`; 403 `forbidden`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

### live

A fragment's live socket: records as they are appended, presence, drafts and queries.

#### `live.open`

`GET /f/{name}/__live`: A fragment's live socket: subscribe to channels, share presence, run queries.

- Credentials: NIP-98, its page's session, its framed page's session, its share link, none
- A WebSocket; sends: [`LiveIn`](#livein)
- A WebSocket; receives: [`LiveOut`](#liveout)
- Refusals ([`ErrorBody`](#errorbody)): 401 `unauthenticated`; 403 `forbidden`; 404 `not_found`; 500 `schema_refused`; 503 `deploying`

### `ListWatchFrame`

out, open. A frame of `GET /api/fragments/watch`, the socket that follows a signer's list. Frames name nothing: on `changed` or `invited` the page reads its list, or its invites, again with its own credential. The cell writes these as constant text (cell/src/principal.rs). Open (`x-fragment-open`, docs/wire): a reader ignores a frame it does not know (the shell's list, the iOS app).

`type: "hello"`. First, as the socket opens.

`type: "changed"`. Their list changed: a row added, gone, or changed.

`type: "invited"`. The invites waiting on their emails changed: one made, renewed or taken back.
