Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Finite (finite.chat) is run by Finite Computer LLC ("we"). This page says what we keep, why, and who else handles it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and how you sign in.
  • What you make: your apps, chats, files and messages, your agents' memories and skills, and your computer's disk (its newest saves).
  • Usage and billing: what you used and what it cost. Stripe holds your card details, not us.
  • Logs: short-lived records our servers write to run and fix the service, kept for about a week.

How we use it

Only to run Finite for you: to store it, show it to you and to the people you share it with, and let your agents work with it.

We don't sell or rent your data. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't train AI models on it.

Who else handles it

These services run parts of Finite. Each gets only what its part needs:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage, your computer, some AI models, and our email.
  • code.storage: stores your apps' files.
  • WorkOS: sign-in, and the accounts you connect (such as Google).
  • Stripe: payments.
  • OpenRouter: sends your agents' requests to AI models, with zero data retention.
  • Tool services: when your agent uses one of these tools, the request goes to that service: X, Firecrawl, fal, Browser Use, Perplexity, xAI, ElevenLabs.
  • Google: only if you connect your Google account (see below).
  • Your browser's push service: only if you turn on notifications. The message is encrypted for your device.

Your agents can also visit websites and use other services when you ask them to. Those see what your agent sends them.

Google data

If you connect Google, your agents use it only to do what you ask. We don't share it, sell it, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it, and we keep only what your agent saves for you. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings, which revokes our access.

Your choices

  • Download everything that's yours from Settings → Your data.
  • Disconnect a connected account in Settings.
  • Delete your account and everything in it by emailing support@finite.computer. We keep payment records only as the law requires.

Cookies

We use one cookie, to keep you signed in. No ads, no tracking.

Children

Finite isn't for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you before it takes effect. Questions: support@finite.computer.
