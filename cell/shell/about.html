About Finite

Finite is an AI assistant you talk to in chat. It gives you agents that work for you, on a computer of your own: they do the computer work, like research, writing, handling your email and calendar, and building small apps for you and the people you share them with. Your computer stays awake, so your agents can keep working while you get on with your day.

Sign in to Finite to start a chat with your agents.

Connecting Google

You can connect your Google account in Settings, choosing read only or read and write. Your agents then use your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and contacts only to do what you ask: find and summarize mail, check your schedule and add events, read and edit the files you name, or look up someone's address. You can disconnect at any time. Our privacy policy says what we keep and who handles it.

Why we built it

We believe in a right ordering of people and computers: humans do human things, and computers do computer things. A computer should make finite demands on your life and attention. You're the hero here, not the AI and not our product.

What you make stays yours. We store it only to run Finite for you: we don't sell your data, show you ads, or train AI models on it. Finite is made by Finite Computer LLC, a small team serving people and organizations who value privacy and self-sovereignty.
