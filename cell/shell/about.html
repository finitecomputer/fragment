About Finite

Finite gives you agents that work for you, on a computer of your own. You talk to them in chat, and they do the computer work: research, writing, handling your email and calendar, and building small apps for you and the people you share them with. Your computer stays awake, so your agents can keep working while you get on with your day.

We believe in a right ordering of people and computers: humans do human things, and computers do computer things. A computer should make finite demands on your life and attention. You're the hero here, not the AI and not our product.

What you make stays yours. We store it only to run Finite for you: we don't sell your data, show you ads, or train AI models on it. Finite is made by Finite Computer LLC, a small team serving people and organizations who value privacy and self-sovereignty.
